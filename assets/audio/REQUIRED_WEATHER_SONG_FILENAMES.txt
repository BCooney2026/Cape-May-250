Put your MP3 files in this folder using these exact names:

REQUIRED:
hot.mp3       = hot weather song, example: Heat Wave
rain.mp3      = rainy weather song, example: Raindrops Keep Fallin' on My Head
windy.mp3     = windy weather song, example: Against the Wind
perfect.mp3   = perfect weather / default vacation song

OPTIONAL BUT SUPPORTED:
cloudy.mp3    = cloudy weather song
storm.mp3     = thunderstorm / stormy weather song
cold.mp3      = chilly weather song

Do not rename these in the page code. Change the filenames here or edit assets/config/vacation-config.js.
