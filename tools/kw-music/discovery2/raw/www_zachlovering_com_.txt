<section id="about" class="sec"><h2>About</h2><div class="split"><figure class="photo tilt-l"><img src="img/looper-rig.jpg" alt="Zach on stage barefoot with his pedals and looper" style="object-position:50% 60%"></figure><div class="card about"><img class="emblem" src="img/emblem.png" alt="">
<p>Zach Lovering is a singer/songwriter who made his way to the Florida Keys in 2015 from Atlanta, GA. His experience being raised in church choirs and theatre stages bleeds into all of his music through his strong and soulful vocals. Accompanied by his guitar and looper, Zach creates a high-energy show that turns familiar songs into uniquely performed renditions.</p>
<p>Finding Zach isn’t hard most weekends, if you’re visiting Key West. He plays at several prominent spots right on Duval St downtown. So if you find yourself itching for some great live music while on vacation, now you know where to go!!</p></div></div></section>
