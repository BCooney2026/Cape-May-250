A little planning goes a long way

Before you
book your ride.

Choose the right capacity, then check the details that shape your rental day.

What should the booking price include?

Check the rental period, taxes, fees, deposit and any insurance options. Compare the final total for the same dates and duration.

What driver and passenger requirements apply?

Confirm the provider’s minimum driver age, license requirements, passenger limits and child seating policies before reserving.

Where are pickup and return?

Review the exact pickup address, opening hours, return time and fuel or charging instructions. Ask the provider about delivery if it matters to your plans.

What about routes and parking?

Ask the provider which roads the rented vehicle is permitted to use and what parking rules apply. Check posted signs at each stop.

Can a cart be booked for today?

Open the provider’s booking calendar and select today’s date to check offered times and availability. A listing on this page does not guarantee a same-day rental.
