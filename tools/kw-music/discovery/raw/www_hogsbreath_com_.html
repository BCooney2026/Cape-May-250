THE HOG’S BREATH LEGACY

From its humble beginnings in Ft. Walton Beach to its iconic status on Duval Street, Hog’s Breath Saloon has been serving up good times, cold drinks, and live music for decades. What started as a local watering hole has become a must-visit destination, and we’re just getting started!

READ OUR STORY

WHERE TASTY BITES MEET ICE COLD DRINKS

The Original Hog’s Breath Saloon was established in Ft. Walton Beach, FL, in 1976. It was the brain child of Jerry Dorminy, a Birmingham, Alabama expatriate who left the mountains for the sun and fun of the gulf coast of Florida. The first order of business was to organize a watering hole where he and his friends could hang out at night after a full day of sailing or fishing. It immediately became the place where all the local watermen and women partied long after dark and on weekends.
